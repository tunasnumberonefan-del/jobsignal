# Short-form video scripts (30 to 45 seconds)

Format for all of them: screen recording of the real product with your voice over it. Face cam optional. Use real listings, but **never imply a real named company is running a scam**. For scam examples, use pasted text you wrote yourself and say so on screen. Blur company names when the point is the pattern, not the employer.

---

## 1. "Is this job even real?"
**Hook (0–3s):** "This job has been 'posted 2 days ago' for five months. Watch."
**Body:** Paste the link. The report loads. Point at the stamp: "Open for 152 days." Scroll to "Ask them": "Is this role still being actively filled?"
**Close:** "Not saying don't apply. Saying ask first. Link in bio, it's free."

## 2. The anti-scam sentence
**Hook:** "One sentence in a job post that protects you from scams."
**Body:** Show a real listing's report with the "Includes the employer's own scam warning" finding: "Recruiters only contact you from @company.com." "If someone messages you about this job from a Gmail, it's not them."
**Close:** "This tool pulls that line out for you. Free, link in bio."

## 3. "Hiring and laying off at the same time"
**Hook:** "This company is hiring. It also filed a layoff notice with the state in August."
**Body:** Show a report with a WARN finding and the timeline. "That's a WARN notice. Companies have to file them before big layoffs. It's public. Almost nobody checks."
**Close:** "Doesn't mean don't apply. Means ask which team got cut. Free tool, link in bio."
*(Only use a real company if the WARN record is accurate and you show the official source on screen.)*

## 4. Fake listing, live test
**Hook:** "I wrote a fake job post with every scam trick I know. Let's see if this catches it."
**Body:** Paste your own scam text (Telegram interview, buy equipment, send SSN). Show "Significant inconsistencies" and the findings.
**Close:** "Then I tried a real listing from a real company." Quick cut to "Strong verification signals". "Free, link in bio."

## 5. The 3-question habit
**Hook:** "Three questions I ask before I spend an hour on an application."
**Body:** 1) Is it live on the company's own job board? 2) How long has it really been up? 3) Has the company had layoffs this year? "This tool answers all three from one link."
**Close:** "Two free checks a day, no account. Link in bio."
