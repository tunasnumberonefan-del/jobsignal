# X (Twitter) post ideas

1. Most "posted 2 days ago" job listings aren't. Aggregators show when they scraped it. The company's own job board has the real date, and JobSignal reads it for you. [link]

2. Job scam tell: the interview happens on Telegram, or they'll "send you a check for equipment". Real employers do neither. I built a free checker that flags these patterns in any listing. [link]

3. Under the WARN Act, larger companies have to file a notice with the state before mass layoffs. It's public. Almost nobody checks it before applying. JobSignal shows it next to the job. [link]

4. Built a job-listing checker that refuses to give you a single "trust score". You get three separate answers: is the posting real, what do public records say about the employer, and how much evidence there was. Faking certainty helps no one. [link]

5. Small thing I'm proud of: when a listing includes the company's own anti-scam notice ("we only email from @acme.com"), JobSignal pulls that out and shows it to you, instead of mistaking it for a scam. [link]
