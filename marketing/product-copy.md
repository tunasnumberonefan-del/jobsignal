# Product copy

## One-liner
JobSignal checks a job posting and the company behind it before you apply.

## Elevator pitch (30 seconds)
Job hunting right now means sending applications into listings that were filled months ago, listings that were never real, and the occasional scam wearing a real company's logo. JobSignal takes a job link and checks it against the company's own job board, tells you how long it has actually been up and whether it keeps getting reposted, flags text that asks for things real employers don't, and pulls layoff notices and SEC filings for the company. Every finding links to where it came from. It's free for a few checks a day.

## Product description (about 120 words)
JobSignal is a free tool for checking job listings before you apply. Paste a link from Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, or a company careers page, and in a few seconds you get three plain answers: does the posting check out, what do public records say about the employer, and how much evidence was there to go on.

It looks at whether the listing is live on the employer's own job board, when it was first published, whether it has been taken down and reposted, and whether the text asks for money, ID, or chat-app interviews. For the employer, it pulls state WARN Act layoff notices and SEC filings. Every finding links to its source, and when the data isn't there, the report says so instead of guessing.

## Landing page copy (as built)
- **Kicker:** For people applying to a lot of jobs
- **Headline:** Stop wasting applications on questionable job listings.
- **Sub:** Paste a job link. We check whether it's live on the employer's own job board, how long it's really been up, whether it keeps getting reposted, and what layoff notices and SEC filings say about the company.
- **Under the form:** Free. No account needed for your first couple of checks.
- **Why this exists:** Some listings stay up for months after the role is filled. Some were never really open. Some are copies of real jobs, posted by someone who wants your bank details. And some companies are hiring at the front door while filing layoff notices out back. None of that is secret. It's just spread across job board APIs, state labor department spreadsheets, and SEC filings that nobody has time to dig through for every application.
- **What we won't do:** We won't call a company a scam, or a listing a ghost job. We won't make up dates. We won't treat no news as good news. We won't hide where things came from.
- **Final CTA:** Got a listing open in another tab? Check it before you spend the evening on the cover letter.

## Words to avoid in all copy
"Guaranteed", "accurate" (as an unqualified claim), "scam detector", "AI-powered", "ghost job score", "safe employer", "revolutionary", "seamless", "empower", "unlock", "game-changer". And no em-dash-heavy, buzzword-stuffed sentences. Write like you'd explain it to a friend.
